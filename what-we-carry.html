What we carry

Exotics only, and a lot of them

No dogs, no cats, no general pet aisle. What we do is reptiles, amphibians, inverts and fish, plus everything it takes to keep them. Stock changes constantly, so the list below is what we usually have rather than a promise about today. Call ahead or just come look!

Snakes

This is the wall people come for. Colubrids, boas, garters and plenty more, mostly smaller snakes with some larger ones mixed in. New morphs come through all the time, so it is worth checking back.

Lizards

Bearded dragons and chameleons are almost always here. Geckos, iguanas, green tree monitors and curl tail lizards come and go, and rarer albino and leucistic morphs turn up now and then.

Frogs and amphibians

Several kinds of frogs at any given time, and axolotls on a regular basis. If you have been waiting on axolotls, keep an eye on our Facebook page, that is where they get announced first.

Invertebrates

Jumping spiders, tarantulas and beetles. We also became a distributor of tarantula enclosures recently, so we can set you up with the animal and the right home for it in one trip.

Turtles and tortoises

Both, usually little ones. They are slower than everything else in the store and a lot of people end up leaving with one anyway.

Feeders

Feeder fish, feeder mice, grubs, crickets and mealworms. People tell us our feeders are the best value around for the quantity you get, and we would rather keep it that way than nickel and dime you on bugs.

Setups and supplies

Enclosures, heat, lighting, substrate and live plants. If you tell us what you are keeping and how much room you have, we will tell you exactly what you need and what you can skip.

Not sure what you are looking at?

That is normal, and asking is free. Come in and we will walk the wall with you and talk through what is easy to keep and what needs a little more work.
