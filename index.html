Ask us anything

First one? Ask away.

Nobody takes home their first gecko without a hundred questions, and that's our favorite part of the job. Ask us about heat, humidity, feeding, or just what's easiest to start with. Come on in and we'll walk you through it!

The ones we get asked most

Do you have a specific animal in stock right now?

Our stock changes constantly, so the honest answer is give us a call at (407) 260-0552 and we will tell you what is on the wall today. What was here last week is probably different from what is here now, and that is half the fun of coming in.

Do you ship animals?

No. Everything we sell goes home with somebody who came in and picked it out. We think that is better anyway. You get to see the animal in person, and we get to answer your questions before you commit to anything.

I have never kept a reptile. What should I start with?

Come ask us, because the right answer depends on you. How much room you have, how much you want to handle it, how much time you want to put in. There are plenty of good beginner animals and we will point you at the one that fits your life, not the most expensive one on the wall.

What is it going to cost to get set up?

Usually more than the animal itself. The enclosure, heat, lighting and substrate are the part people underestimate. Tell us what you are thinking about keeping and we will give you a straight number before you buy anything.

How big is this going to get?

Ask us before you buy, not after. It is the question that trips people up most and the easiest one to answer early. Everything on the wall has a grown-up size and we will tell you honestly what you are signing up for.

Do you sell feeders?

We do. Feeder fish, feeder mice, grubs, crickets and mealworms. People tell us our feeders are the best value around for the quantity you get, and we would rather keep it that way than nickel and dime you on bugs.

Can I buy supplies if I got my animal somewhere else?

Of course. We are happy to help whether it came from us or not. Bring a photo of your setup and we will tell you what is working and what is not.

Do you carry anything besides reptiles?

Plenty. Frogs and amphibians, axolotls when we have them, jumping spiders, beetles, tarantulas, turtles and tortoises, and feeder fish. If it is a little unusual, there is a decent chance we have it or have had it.

What are your hours and where are you?

Every day, 10 AM to 8 PM, closed major holidays. We are at 490 State Road 436, which people also call Semoran Boulevard, in Casselberry.

How long have you been around?

Since 1985. Same store, same corner, a lot of the same customers.
